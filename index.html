The Problem

Millions of Nigerian tailors run on notebooks and memory.

And they lose money every single month. Not because they lack skill — because they have no system.

📏

Wrong Measurements

Tape measuring takes 20–30 minutes, and errors still happen. When they do, the fabric is cut, the work is done, and the outfit doesn't fit.

Cost: ₦15,000–₦60,000 per remake
💬

Client Disputes

"This isn't what I asked for." There's no proof of what was agreed. The tailor either remakes it for free or gives a discount.

Cost: Free remake + damaged reputation
💰

Unpaid Debts

Clients owe money. The tailor writes it in a notebook and forgets. Weeks later, the money is gone and it's too awkward to ask.

Cost: ₦50,000–₦500,000/month lost
📱

"Is It Ready?"

Twenty WhatsApp messages a day from anxious clients. Each one pulls the tailor off the sewing machine. Hours lost, every week.

Cost: 10–15 hours lost per week
📊

No Profit Visibility

The tailor counts cash at night. But between restocking, expenses, and family withdrawals — is the business actually profitable?

Cost: Business runs blind
🌐

No Online Presence

Instagram profile isn't a website. The tailor can't be found on Google. Higher-paying clients go to competitors who look more professional.

Cost: Lost premium clients

"Your notebook is free. But how much is it costing you every month?"

— The conversation that sells Vyora

The Solution

Vyora gives every tailor a complete digital business.

Not another tracker. A system that prevents the losses, manages the work, and markets the business.

Killer Feature
🤖

AI Body Measurement

Take 2 photos of a client. Vyora generates 80+ precise measurements in 10 seconds. No tape. No errors. No more remakes because of a wrong measurement.

Killer Feature
✅

Design Approval System

Before cutting fabric, the client approves the design on your phone. Silhouette, neckline, sleeves, fabric, reference photos. Timestamped. Locked. If they dispute later, you show them the proof.

📋

Order & Client Management

Every client, every order, every stage — from cutting to sewing to fitting to delivery. Searchable forever. Nothing gets lost.

💵

Invoices & Payment Tracking

Generate professional invoices. Record every payment. See exactly who owes what. Every morning, Vyora emails you a list of debts to chase.

🌐

Your Own Website

Every tailor gets a public page at vyora.ng/your-name. Portfolio, pricing, contact — a real website you can share on Instagram or WhatsApp. No coding, no cost.

🧻

Fabric Inventory

Track fabric by type, colour, and quantity. Alerts when stock is low. Dead stock reports. Stop tying up money in fabric you're not using.

👥

Staff & Shift Management

Add staff, assign roles, set PINs. Track every sale and every shift. Know exactly who did what, and catch discrepancies before they grow.

📈

Profit Dashboard

Today's revenue, this month's profit, top clients, best sellers. Real-time numbers, not guesswork.

📱

Works Like an App

Installable on any phone. Works on iPhone, Android, Windows, and Mac. No downloads from app stores. Just your browser.
